# Third-party methods and formatting

The report uses the official [NeurIPS 2026 author template](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers) in **preprint** mode. This independent report has not been submitted to or accepted by NeurIPS. The template is fetched from its official host and checked against `report/style.sha256`; the style file is not redistributed here. Its authors retain attribution in that file.

Architectures are small experimental implementations using PyTorch. The report cites HyperNetworks, Relation Networks and AdamW as relevant prior work. Our relational module and input-conditioned hypernetwork are adaptations, not claimed replications of those papers.

No repository-wide reuse license has been selected yet. Public availability does not change third-party licenses.
